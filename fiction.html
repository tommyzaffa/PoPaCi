[ Fiction · Experimental ]

Nothing But Flesh

Director
Dario Cirrincione, Gabriele Crasci
Production
PoPaCi Pictures SNC
Producer
Liam Pagnamenta
Music Composer
Alessandro Bavari
AI Artist
Dario Cirrincione
Editor
Gabriele Crasci

An experimental film created entirely using artificial intelligence. The project stems from an attempt to take a closer look at how algorithms construct images and, consequently, influence the way we perceive the world.

The film unfolds as a visual experience that traverses constantly shifting forms, bodies and identities. Artificial intelligence becomes part of the discourse itself, highlighting the mechanisms of repetition, selection and standardisation that underpin the systems we use every day. It prompts reflection on what it means today to look, to recognise oneself and to stand out in a context increasingly mediated by algorithms.
