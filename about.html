About.

Three founders, four collaborators, one shared frame. Below: the people who make the company breathe.

[ Founders ]

The founders.

  • 01

    Dario Cirrincione

    Director · Producer

    Founding eye of PoPaCi. Trained between Milan and Geneva, Dario directs the company's flagship fiction and curates the Merge Film Festival. Believes the camera should ask the question, not answer it.

  • 02

    Francesco Poloni

    Director · Producer

    Documentary director and co-founder. Francesco's work explores invisible borders — geographic, political, personal. His films have travelled from Locarno to IDFA.

  • 03

    Liam Pagnamenta

    Producer

    Producer and operational backbone of the company. Liam built PoPaCi's co-production architecture across Switzerland, Italy and the EU, and runs the day-to-day of every set we step on.
