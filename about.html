[ Founders ]

The founders.

  • 01

    Dario Cirrincione

    Director · Writer · Producer

    Born in Basel, Switzerland, in 2003, Dario is a Swiss director, writer and producer. In 2022 he began his studies at the International Conservatory of Audiovisual Sciences (CISA), where he obtained a diploma in Visual Design in 2024 and graduated in film directing in 2025. In his work he explores hybrid languages and contemporary formats, with a particular focus on integrating artificial intelligence into creative processes. His graduation short film Present was selected for several international festivals, including the World AI Film Festival, where it won the award in the ‘8th Art Film’ section. He serves as General Director and Artistic Director of the Merge Film Festival and is co-founder of PoPaCi Pictures SNC.

  • 02

    Francesco Poloni

    Director · Writer

    Born in 1997 in Bellinzona, Switzerland, Francesco is a Swiss filmmaker and writer of Italian origin. After initially studying psychology and architecture, he graduated in film directing from CISA — the International Conservatory of Audiovisual Sciences in Locarno — in 2025. In his work he explores the relationships between the individual, space and context, maintaining a minimalist, observation-based approach. He directed Tusen Toner (2025), a graduation short film selected for the Locarno Film Festival's Pardi di domani — National Competition section, where it won the SRG SSR Silver Pardino. He serves as Operations Director and Artistic Director of the Merge Film Festival and is co-founder of PoPaCi Pictures SNC.

  • 03

    Liam Pagnamenta

    Producer

    Born in Lugano in 2003, Liam is a Swiss producer working in the audiovisual sector. Following his business studies at the Cantonal School of Commerce in Bellinzona, he oversees the production, organisational and financial aspects of the projects he manages. At the same time, he works as an audiovisual programmer. He serves as Financial Director and Artistic Director of the Merge Film Festival and is co-founder of PoPaCi Pictures SNC, through which he develops film and art projects as well as cultural initiatives in Switzerland and on the international stage.
