[ 001 ] About

PoPaCi Pictures is an audiovisual production company based in Ticino, founded on 28 January 2026, specialising in the development and production of short and feature-length films.

Our work extends beyond film production to include the promotion of audiovisual culture through the organisation of events and the design of installations. Our work forms part of a broader field of research that explores the relationship between image, technology and contemporary society, with a particular focus on the implications of artificial intelligence.
