[ 001 ] About

PoPaCi Pictures is an independent production house based between Lugano and the rest of the world. We build films, festivals and frameworks where cinema meets technology — without losing the human pulse that started it all.

From feature fiction to long-form documentary, from festival curation to AI-driven storytelling research, every project we sign carries the same obsession: a frame that earns its silence.
