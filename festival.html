Merge.

Switzerland's first AI film festival — where artificial intelligence meets cinema.

[ What it is ]

Merge Film Festival is an international festival dedicated to films created with or about artificial intelligence.

Born from the vision of PoPaCi Pictures, Merge is conceived as a space for experimentation, dialogue and public engagement at the intersection of cinema and technology. It aims to map the cultural and perceptual implications of AI — not just as a production tool, but as a subject of artistic and critical investigation.

The festival brings together filmmakers, researchers and audiences to explore how algorithms reshape the way we construct, consume and interpret images. It is the first festival of its kind in Switzerland.
