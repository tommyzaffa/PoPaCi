Legal

Privacy Policy.

We keep things minimal: no tracking, no cookies, no analytics. This page explains the few pieces of data we handle and why.

Last updated: 19 May 2026

[ 01 ] Data controller

Who we are.

This website (www.popaci.com) is operated by PoPaCi Pictures SNC, based in Lugano, Ticino, Switzerland. For any privacy-related request you can write to info@popaci.com.

[ 02 ] What we collect

Personal data.

Browsing this website does not require you to provide any personal data. We do not use cookies, local storage, session storage, advertising IDs or analytics scripts. We do not profile visitors and we do not build behavioural records of any kind.

The only personal data we receive is what you voluntarily send us through the contact form or by email:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • The subject you select
  • The content of your message

Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (IP address, timestamp, requested URL, user agent) for security and operational purposes. These logs are kept only for as long as needed to detect abuse and are not used to identify or track visitors.

[ 03 ] Purpose & legal basis

Why we use it.

We use the data you send us for one purpose only: to read your message and reply. The legal basis is your consent, given by submitting the form, and our legitimate interest in answering enquiries about our work (Art. 31 Swiss FADP and, where applicable, Art. 6(1)(a) and (f) GDPR).

We never use your data for marketing, never sell it to third parties, never enrol you in mailing lists without an explicit opt-in.

[ 04 ] Third parties

Who else processes data.

To keep the website running we rely on a small number of external services. Each acts as an independent processor under its own privacy terms:

  • Formspree (United States) — receives messages submitted through the contact form and forwards them to our inbox. See Formspree Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts (Ireland / United States) — serves the web fonts used by the site. Your browser connects to Google's servers, which may log your IP address. See Google Privacy Policy.
  • Our hosting provider — stores the website files and serves the pages you visit.

Some of these providers are located outside Switzerland and the EU. Where this involves an international transfer of personal data, it takes place under appropriate safeguards (standard contractual clauses or equivalent mechanisms adopted by the providers).

[ 05 ] Retention

How long we keep it.

Messages sent through the contact form are kept in our inbox for as long as is needed to handle your request and any reasonable follow-up, then archived or deleted. You can ask us at any time to delete the conversation.

[ 06 ] Cookies

Cookies.

This website does not set any cookies of its own and does not store anything in your browser's local storage. No consent banner is shown because none is required.

[ 07 ] Your rights

Your rights.

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and — where applicable — the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request its correction or deletion
  • Restrict or object to its processing
  • Withdraw your consent at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority (in Switzerland: the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner — edoeb.admin.ch)

To exercise any of these rights, just email us at info@popaci.com. We will reply within a reasonable timeframe.

[ 08 ] Changes

Updates to this policy.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or in the law. The date of the latest revision is shown at the top of this page.
