Liam Pagnamenta

Founder · Producer

Born in Lugano in 2003, Liam is a Swiss producer working in the audiovisual sector. Following his business studies at the Cantonal School of Commerce in Bellinzona, he oversees the production, organisational and financial aspects of the projects he manages.

At the same time, he works as an audiovisual programmer. He serves as Financial Director of the Merge Film Festival and is co-founder of PoPaCi Pictures SNC, through which he develops film and art projects as well as cultural initiatives in Switzerland and on the international stage.
